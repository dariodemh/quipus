1 · The Methodological Problem

Why a Heterogeneous Corpus Misleads Without Strict Null Models

Across 650+ khipus and tens of thousands of numbers and colors, almost any post-hoc rule yields seemingly “significant” coincidences.

1. Chance Sums

Medrano & Khosla's (2024) 74% counts Ascher relations accurately, but shuffling khipu-by-khipu reproduces 93%–100% of them by chance (~24% power).

2. Leakage & Pseudoreplication

Treating cords as independent samples or validating without grouping by site/source inflates p to 10−267 and hides recorder effects (CREATED_BY).

3. Our v2 Protocol

SHA-256 sealed preregistrations before running code, adversarial pre-audit (Opus), pure held-out split (41 khipus), and empirical FPR calibration.
