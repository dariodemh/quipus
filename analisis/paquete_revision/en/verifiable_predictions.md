# Verifiable predictions

This document collects concrete predictions that we cannot check from the digital data. Seeing the object, a photograph with a scale, or the archival document is required. **We have not seen any of those objects or documents.**

Each prediction states:

- what is predicted;
- on which object or document;
- which result would confirm it and which would refute it;
- where it comes from and since when it has been fixed.

Unless stated otherwise, the predictions were fixed on 18-10-2026 in the cited files, before any external contact.

**General warning.** At Inkawasi, KFG and OKR derive from the **same reading** (the same transcriber). An independent physical re-reading is, on its own, the test we most need.

Notation: `p180` is pendant cord no. 180 in the KFG/OKR numbering; `s1` is its first subsidiary cord. In the knots, `7L` is a long knot of 7 turns, `5S` a cluster of 5 simple knots, and `E` a figure-of-eight knot (value 1).

## A. Inkawasi: specific cords (current custodian of the khipus from A. Chu's excavations)

Priority, from highest to lowest: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7. The first four are the cheapest and most informative.

**1. UR278 p180 (group g21, column 1).**

- **Prediction:** the last long knot has **6 turns**, not 7. The value would be 237, not 238.
- **Why:**
  - the cell must equal the pendant cord plus subsidiary cord of UR270 g0: 178 + 59 = 237;
  - the g21 row sums to 1654, and the book (UR266/UR275) gives 1653.

  A single change fixes both checks at once.
- **Confirms:** 6L. **Refutes:** a clear, intact 7L. In that case, the two +1 discrepancies are real, i.e., the original record already differed.
- *Source:* `ds/n_casi_aciertos/informe.md` §3.3c; `19_prediccion/predicciones_museo.csv`.

**2. UR275 p51 (quota of record g10).**

- **Prediction:** the cord is worth 47 (`4S,40; 7L,7`) and the terminal 7L was lost or not transcribed. It is currently recorded as 40.
- **Why:** the other 24 quotas of the khipu are worth 47, and p51 measures 26.5 cm, against a median of 41.8 cm.
- **Confirms:** a 7L, or a broken tip just after the tens cluster. **Refutes:** an intact tip with no units knot.
- *Source:* `ds/n_casi_aciertos` §3.3e; `19_prediccion` §6.

**3. UR274B p9, p13, p19 and p64 (quotas recorded as 10).**

- **Prediction:** they are quotas of **17** (`1S,10; 7L,7`) missing the terminal 7L.
- **Why:**
  - the other 12 quotas of the khipu are worth 17;
  - the four "10"s are among the five shortest of the 16 cords worth 10 or 17 (20.5–25.0 cm);
  - 1332 = 1315 + 17.
- **Confirms:** the 7L or remains of it, or broken tips. **Refutes:** four intact tips with no units knot. Then the quota 10 is a genuine variant of the record, and the 17 of UR274B is weakened.
- *Source:* `ds/n_casi_aciertos` §3.3f; checked in `paquete_revision/verificar.py` (corrects «exactamente las 4 más cortas»).

**4. UR278 p77 (group g8, cell 5).**

- **Prediction:** a terminal **6L** is missing; the value would be 346, not 340.
- **Why:** the g8 row sums to 3243 and the book gives 3249 (+6), and p77 is the only one of the 9 cells without a units knot.
- **Confirms:** 6L, or a broken tip after the tens. **Refutes:** an intact tip.
- *Source:* `ds/n_casi_aciertos` §3.4.

**5. Hundreds cluster of the same total in four objects.**

- **Prediction:** UR269 p181 has 4 hundreds knots (2433), UR266 p64 and UR275 p41 have 6 (2633), and one cell of the g12 row of UR278 (p109–p117) has one more hundreds knot than transcribed.
- **Why:** the g12 row sums to 2533 and the books give 2633. The copies differ by 4, 5 and 6 hundreds knots.
- **Confirms:** a cell of g12 with one more hundreds knot; then the row sums to 2633 and the discrepancy was a transcription error. **Refutes:** the four objects as transcribed; the difference between copies is real and shows how the records were copied.
- *Warning:* 2633 also appears in UR269 p89, so the "ladder" is not necessarily linear.
- *Source:* `ds/n_casi_aciertos` §3.3a; work log (SINTESIS.md, Spanish only) `ds/n_tanteo` §8.

**6. UR266 p104.**

- **Prediction:** it is worth **1737** (`1S,1000; 7S,700; 3S,30; 7L,7`). This is what the sum 448 + 1289 of UR273A and UR273B gives in the g11 record.
- **Why it needs checking:** Urton (2017) cites 1,732. The OKR was reimported in 2020 and now says 1737.
- **Confirms:** 7L. **Refutes:** 2L, i.e., 1732. Then one of the 8 exact sums of H1 disappears.
- *Source:* `18_equipo_rojo/informe.md` (data flag).

**7. Independent re-reading of complete UR273A, UR273B and UR274A.**

- **Prediction:** a new reading, made without knowledge of our figures, reproduces the 8 exact A+B sums: 2950, 1653, 1737, 1827, 1780, 2247, 1765 and 1506. It also reproduces the 3 fillers of UR274A (1278, 2020 and 2106).
- **Confirms:** that 7 or more of the 8 hold. **Refutes:** that 3 or more break. In that case H1, currently supported and not demonstrated, falls.
- *Technical detail:* the pink separators should also be recorded. Our alignment depends on them falling in the same groups (10, 14, 16, 23 and 27) in both halves.

**8. Three discrepancies that are NOT single-knot errors.**

- **Prediction:** they will not be resolved by finding one misread knot. They will require a missing cord, two simultaneous errors, or acceptance of a real difference in the record.
- **The cases:**
  - UR278 g27 p217: 246, versus 290 in UR270;
  - UR278 g28 p228: 300, versus 394;
  - UR266 p86: 2114, versus 2020.

  No edit of a cluster produces differences of 44 or 94. We add g4 of UR278: the row differs by +24.
- **Refutes:** a single knot that, re-read, closes the relation.
- *Source:* `ds/n_casi_aciertos` §4–5.

**9. Cords recorded "without knots".**

- **Prediction:** if they retain traces of knots, their values will be the ones we predict:
  - UR255: p2 = 91, p10 = 45, p22 = 70, p25 = 145, p28 = 99, p31 = 126, p34 = 172, p38 = 80 and p41 = 200 (net = header − 15);
  - UR255 p8 and p83 = 15; UR256 p40, p81 and p112 = 10; UR274A p13, p51, p59, p72 and p75 = 30; UR275 p110 = 47.

- *Source:* `19_prediccion/predicciones_museo.csv`, high confidence (43 rows).

## B. Inkawasi: unstudied objects

**10. The five Inkawasi khipus that Urton did not analyse.**

- **Prediction:** Urton and Chu 2015 refer to 34 khipus, but the appendix lists only 29. UR269 is organized into blocks of 5 rows with quotas 47, 46, 46, 44 and 25. If those blocks consolidate five flows, then in the missing khipus (or in unpublished field records) there will be **A/B pairs whose quotas sum to 46 or 44**, and their sums A_i + B_i will give the headers of rows 2–4 of the UR269 blocks.
- **Frozen rule (pre-registered):** align by group number; a blank record in A is filled from its copy.
- **Confirms:** 3 or more exact sums on headers of rows 2–4 of UR269. **Refutes:** no pair, or no exact sum.
- *Source:* `13_sistema_incahuasi/informe.md` §predicción; `18_equipo_rojo` §4; `ds/n_fuera_muestra`.

**11. Another 9-column table at Inkawasi (chili or bean).**

- **Prediction:** if the 9 columns are generic social units (pachacas of a guaranga; *pachaca* = local unit of colonial administration, *guaranga* = larger grouping of pachacas), a 9-column table of another product will show **the same ordered profile**: c1 ≈ c2 are the largest (about 28% of the total between the two), c8 is the smallest, and the correlation with position lies between −0.6 and −0.8.
- **Confirms:** the same profile. **Refutes:** a different profile. In that case the 9 units are specific to the peanut flow.
- *Source:* `ds/n_nueve_partes` §9; `ds/n_nueve_unidades`.

**12. Khipus from sector B (PAI-2016-1 to 4 and 1464; Jiménez 2021) and the JC lot.**

- **Prediction:** they will **not** connect arithmetically to the chain of sector A. This is what happens with the 23 JC khipus of sector B: Z twist direction and small values.
- **Refutes:** exact sums or copies pointing to UR266, UR275 or UR269.
- *Source:* `13_sistema_incahuasi` (negatives); work log (SINTESIS.md, Spanish only), first round of agents.

**13. Huacones-Vilcahuasi, under Cañete (Barraza, Areche and Marcone 2022).**

- **Prediction (speculative, low priority):** if khipus 8 and 9, each with 9 pendant cords, record the same 9 units, their values will decline with position (ρ < −0.5) and will have a CV close to 0.2.
- **Refutes:** values without order or very unequal (CV > 0.5).
- *Source:* work log (SINTESIS.md, Spanish only), «Búsqueda de Chu y del valle de Cañete».

## C. Colonial documents

**14. Bambamarca, visita (colonial inspection) of González de Cuenca (1567).**

- **Prediction:** in the manuscript, or in the Espinoza Soriano edition of 1976–77, the 9 pachacas of the guaranga of Bambamarca appear **in the same order** as in table 2 of Pärssinen 1992, with the principal curaca first and the tributaries 104, 75, 78, 68, 49, 75, 67, 30 and 27.
- **Confirms:** the same order. **Refutes:** another order in the document. Then the decline (ρ = −0.87) is an editorial artefact, and the resemblance to UR278 falls away.
- *Source:* `ds/n_parssinen` §7.

**15. List of pachacas of Lunahuaná or Guarco.**

- **Where to look:** AGI Justicia 432 and 482; Escribanía de Cámara 498-B.
- **Prediction:** if the columns of UR278 are pachacas of a local guaranga, some guaranga in the valley will have **9 pachacas**, or a structure of 4 pairs plus 1 under 5 curacas (local lords). Their relative sizes will be moderately unequal (maximum/minimum between 1.5 and 2.5; CV between 0.15 and 0.4), and the names will follow the order of rank.
- **Confirms:** a guaranga of 9 pachacas with those sizes. **Refutes:** documented guarangas of 8 or 10 pachacas with none of 9. Rostworowski gives for Lunahuaná 4 guarangas and 8 curacas.
- *Source:* `ds/n_rostworowski` §8; `ds/n_nueve_partes` §9.

**16. Visita of Guancayo (Chillón, 1571; AGI Justicia 413).**

- **Prediction:** in the manuscript, the order of the curacas is Mongoy (2 pachacas), Cancay (1), Chuquitanga (2), Chumbillán (2) and Antachumbi (2), as in the transcription of Espinoza 1963. Two doubts are also resolved: «cinco» versus «nueve» pachacas, and Maca with 170 or 190.
- **Confirms:** that order and 9 pachacas. **Refutes:** another order. Then the column pairing (1-2)(4-5)(6-7)(8-9) + c3 loses its documentary anchor.
- *Source:* `ds/n_espinoza1963` §10.

**17. Any colonial list of 9 units dictated from a khipu.**

- **Control prediction (a risk to the model):** lists read from khipus that follow an administrative order will, for the most part, be ranked by prestige or hierarchy and **not** by size (median ρ ≈ −0.3). If many lists of 9 ordered from largest to smallest appeared, the resemblance to Bambamarca would stop being special.
- *Source:* `ds/n_parssinen`.

## D. Other khipus

**18. UR053A–E (Banco Central de Reserva del Perú, Lima): W / R / AB split.**

- **Prediction:**
  - in a re-reading with the colors checked, the W cord is the largest of the trio in at least 85% of the trios of each khipu;
  - the mean split is 0.55–0.72 / 0.15–0.27 / 0.12–0.20;

- **Confirms:** that split in all five. **Refutes:** that in some khipu R or AB is the largest in more than 30% of the trios, or that the red "R" turn out to be different colors (RL versus others) with different splits.
- *Source:* work log (SINTESIS.md, Spanish only), «UR053A–E como archivo»; `21_ur053/alinear.py`; checked in `verificar.py`.

**21. Khipus with canutos (cord wrappings/sleeves) (UR039, UR050, UR054, UR055, UR110, UR112, UR144).**

## F. Boundary cases, copies and mismatches (rounds 17–18)

These predictions were fixed on 18-10-2026, after the data had been inspected (post hoc). They are weaker than those in sections A–E for that reason.

**27. UR234 g5, broken header (Ethnologisches Museum, Berlin).**

- **Prediction:** the broken header of group 5 is worth 2016 (`2S,2000; 1S,10; 6L,6`).
- **Why:** UR1104 (AS104, Berlin, Pachacamac) reproduces in order the headers of UR234 g0–g8 (2007, 2080, 3102, 2247, 2578, **2016**, 3419, 3159, 1407; UR234: 2007, 2080, 3012, 2247, 2578, broken, 3419, 3159, 1407). There are 7 exact matches, one variant (3102 versus 3012: the same knot in another decimal position) and the broken header. It is the «extracto de un campo» (field extract) type.
- **Confirms:** remains of knots or a trace of 2016 on the broken cord. **Refutes:** another legible value. Additionally: re-read UR234 g2 (3012 or 3102?) and the same header in UR1104.
- *Source:* `analisis/33_estematica/informe.md`; `verificar.py` (check 11).

**28. UR108 (Lima, Temple Radicati) versus cord 9 of UR231 (Berlin), and the attachment VA16135a/b.**

- **Prediction:** the values of UR108 coincide with cord 9 in 5 of the 6 large sections of UR231 (5/6 exact); UR108 would be a field extract, like UR1104.
- **Level:** proposed (post hoc). UR108 has only 7 cords, so the order contributes little (1/60).
- **Confirms:** that the sixth section also coincides on re-reading. If it holds, it supports VA16135a and VA16135b being a single khipu (the physical attachment must be checked in the museum). **Refutes:** that the five coincidences are due to misreadings.

**29. UR122 (Göteborg 1938.45.0228) versus KH0049 (Lima, MNAAHP3550).**

- **Prediction 1:** cords of UR122 at 0 where KH0049 has a value are **broken or loose** on the object. In the block p19–p36 there are 12 such positions; the first three are p21–p23 (KH0049: 487, 281, 661).

- *Source:* `analisis/30_frontera_estructura/07_ur122_kh0049.py`, `novedad_kh0049.md`; `verificar.py` (check 10).

**30. Re-reading of UR040 (Peabody) and UR167 (AMNH).**

- **Prediction:** the two-color alternation A/B/A/B (W/AB in UR040: 26 of 33 positions; AB/MB in UR167: 30 of 37) is preserved in another reading. The values are almost all 0 or 1, so what must be checked is the same colors in the same positions, not the value.

**31. UR278: long knots in the cells with ±1 mismatch.**

- **Prediction:** in rows g13, g17, g19, g21 and g29 of UR278 (book − detail mismatch of +1, −1, +1, −1, −1) the error lies in the **turn count of a units long knot**. 6 of the 7 mismatches fall on the units (p ≈ 0.007, approximately null). The turns of the long knots in those rows must be counted again. The candidates per row (18–43 per row, except g21) are in `analisis/ds/n_casi_aciertos/informe.md` and `reparaciones.csv`; g21 is already covered by prediction 1.
- **Confirms:** turn counts that, corrected in one cell, make the row sum match the book. **Refutes:** clear, complete long knots in all cells; the discrepancy would then be a real one in the record.
- The other two mismatches (g8, +6; g12, +100) are not of this type: see prediction 4 and the 4/5/6 ladder in `n_casi_aciertos` §3.3a.
- *Source:* `analisis/34_comparada/desajustes.py`; `verificar.py` (check 12).

**32. UR278 g29 (1507) versus g1 (1506): duplicate record?**

- **Prediction:** the g29 row of UR278 sums to 1507 and links to UR266 g2 = 1506, which is already the exact target of g1. g29 is expected to be a **duplicate** of g1 (a group tied or copied twice) or to have one extra knot.

Note: we have not compared g29 with g1 cell by cell; this is a prediction based on the shared target.
